BrowserSnaps Privacy

Last updated: August 13, 2026

BrowserSnaps processes captures locally. It does not transmit page content, URLs, screenshots, or generated files to the developer or any third party.

Information handled

After you start a capture, BrowserSnaps processes the active page title and URL, same-origin navigation links, visibly rendered page content, your capture settings, generated screenshots, and capture metadata. Anything visibly rendered on a signed-in or private page may appear in the screenshot you requested.

Purpose

The information is used only to discover pages you select; render, scroll, capture, stitch, preview, and export screenshots; and restore the original tab state. It is not used for advertising, analytics, profiling, lending, or an unrelated purpose.

Storage and retention

Capture sessions are stored locally in IndexedDB for the results viewer and automatically expire after 24 hours. Files you download remain under your control in the location you choose. BrowserSnaps has no accounts or device synchronization.

Permissions

  • activeTab and scripting operate on the page where you invoke BrowserSnaps.
  • debugger in Chromium builds applies selected responsive viewport sizes and captures tiles in optional dedicated-window mode.
  • offscreen in Chromium builds stitches tiles and prepares local files after the popup closes.
  • downloads saves exports that you request.

Firefox and Safari builds do not request debugger or offscreen. They use visible-tab capture, resize a dedicated capture window for responsive widths, and process results in their extension background context.

BrowserSnaps does not request broad host permissions such as <all_urls>.

Your control

Capture begins only when you select Capture selected pages. You choose the pages and sizes, can cancel a job, can exclude results before export, and can remove the extension or clear its stored data through Chrome settings.

Limited Use

The use of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Read the complete public policy or request support in the BrowserSnaps GitHub repository.
